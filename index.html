Happy Tails Holiday Care — Pet-Sitting Service Agreement

Slogan: You Go. They’re Loved. You Come Home to Happy Tails.

1. Agreed care: We will provide only the services and visit times agreed in writing: feeding and fresh water, companionship, basic tidy-up of ordinary pet mess, and agreed plant watering/mail collection. Medication is provided only if agreed beforehand with clear written instructions.

2. Price and payment: One visit of up to 30 minutes: R120. Two visits per day: R220. Extra pet: R25 per pet per visit. Plant/mail add-on: R20 per visit. A parent/guardian confirms total payment before work starts.

3. Owner responsibilities: The owner will provide accurate care information, sufficient food/supplies, vet details, and safe access instructions.

4. Emergencies: If a pet appears unwell, the sitter will contact the owner/emergency contact promptly. If unreachable, the adult supervisor may arrange veterinary assistance at the owner's expense.

5. Privacy notice (POPIA): We collect only relevant details (name, contact, address, pet routine, emergency contact) to deliver the requested service and manage bookings securely.
